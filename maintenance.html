What it does

Everything a shop's maintenance program actually needs

Not a checklist app bolted on after the fact — a real asset register and PM engine built for a shop that runs machines every day.

TAG

Asset Register

Every machine tagged with a unique asset code and serial number — searchable, retireable, and tied to every task and repair logged against it.

PM

Preventive Maintenance Engine

Recurring PM tasks per asset, with a due list that surfaces automatically — nothing to remember, nothing tracked on a clipboard.

CAL

Maintenance Calendar

A rolling calendar view of what's due and what's coming — the same way project milestones show up on the production schedule.

HRS

Hour-Meter Triggers

Service intervals that fire off actual run hours, not just calendar days — for equipment that wears by use, not by the clock.

REP

Repair History & Downtime

Every repair logged against the asset it happened to, so you can see a machine's full history — and what its downtime has actually cost.

ALT

Safety Alerts

A maintenance issue can be flagged major or minor with a safety callout — so a real hazard doesn't sit quietly in a routine ticket queue.

$

Lifetime Cost per Asset

Parts, labor, and downtime roll up into one running number per machine — the real cost of keeping it, not just what it cost to buy.

SHR

Shared, Multi-User Data

One shared record everyone on the floor and in the office reads from — not a spreadsheet on one machine that only shows its own day.

How it works

From "tag the machine" to "know its full history"

1

Tag the asset

Every machine gets an asset code and serial number the first time it's added to the register — from a table saw to a dust collector.

2

Set the maintenance schedule

Pick calendar-based service (every 90 days) or hour-meter-based service (every 500 run hours) — whichever actually matches how that machine wears.

3

The due list builds itself

Nobody has to remember. What's due — and what's overdue — surfaces on its own, the same way a billing application or a submittal shows up when it's ready.

4

Log the work, flag what's unsafe

A completed task or repair gets logged against the asset. Anything that's a real hazard gets flagged major or minor so it doesn't wait quietly in line.

5

Cost rolls up automatically

Every logged repair adds to that asset's lifetime cost — visible on its own, and available to roll into the same margin reporting the rest of the platform already does.

Why not just use a whiteboard, or a separate CMMS?

One more login is one more silo

A dedicated CMMS product will do some of this too — but it's yet another system, another login, and another database that doesn't know what your production schedule already knows.
