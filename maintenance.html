What it does

Everything a shop's maintenance program actually needs

Not a checklist app bolted on after the fact — an MES-integrated asset register and PM engine built for a shop that runs machines every day.

QR

QR-Tagged Asset Register

Every machine and vehicle is tagged with a QR code. Scan it from any mobile device to open its history, start a work order, or log a daily shift check.

MES

MES & Production Sync

Maintenance doesn't live in a vacuum. PMs sync directly with the manufacturing schedule, ensuring scheduled downtime minimizes impact to actual production.

LOG

Operator Daily Checks

Shift starts with a QR scan. Operators complete daily safety and operational logs (like forklift checks). A failed check instantly flags the maintenance manager.

ALT

Operator-Triggered Issue Logs

Production workers can scan a machine, signal it’s down, and start an issue log that instantly feeds the maintenance queue and flags the production schedule.

PO

Integrated Procurement

Need a spare part? Scan the machine, check the integrated inventory, and trigger a quick Purchase Order straight to the procurement module.

VND

Automated Vendor Routing

When an asset fails a check, the system automatically queues an internal work order—or instantly drafts a request to your preferred external vendor.

SAF

Closed-Loop Safety & Repair

Safety flags halt production. Technicians log the fix, attach photo evidence of repair, and clear the safety block in real-time, leaving a permanent audit trail.

$

Lifetime Cost & Downtime

Parts, labor, and downtime roll up into one running number per machine — the real cost of keeping it, not just what it cost to buy.

How it works

A connected lifecycle, from daily check to final repair

1

Scan to start the shift

Operators use a wireless device to scan an asset's QR code (like a forklift) at the start of their shift. This instantly opens the required daily safety and operational log.

2

Operators signal issues instantly

If a daily check fails, or if a machine breaks mid-shift, production workers flag it as "down." This instantly alerts the maintenance manager, flags the production schedule, and creates a work order tagged for safety hazards.

3

Procure parts and auto-route vendors

The failure triggers action: the system queues an internal ticket or automatically routes an alert to your preferred external vendor. If a spare part is needed internally, technicians scan the machine and create a quick PO in the procurement platform instantly.

4

Fix and prove safety

Maintenance performs the fix, logging their time and attaching photo evidence of the repair directly to the work order. This clears the safety block and re-authorizes the asset for production use.

5

Plan PMs around production (MES Sync)

For preventive maintenance, the scheduled downtime talks to the MES. PMs are planned intelligently around active manufacturing jobs to ensure a routine oil change doesn't blow up a client's delivery date.

MES & Procurement Integration

Feed live equipment data back into the system

Beyond scheduled PM tasks, a machine can report its own condition back into its asset record as it happens — so a problem shows up the moment it starts, not at the next scheduled check.

📡

Every machine and every plant is wired differently

Real-time condition monitoring — sensor, PLC, or hour-meter data streaming back into the asset record automatically — is genuinely possible on this platform, but it's highly custom: what data your equipment can actually expose, how it's networked, and what should trigger an alert all differ machine to machine and plant to plant. This isn't a checkbox we turn on — our consulting team scopes it with you, equipment by equipment.
