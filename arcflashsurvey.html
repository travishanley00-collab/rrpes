Module Features

Everything an electrical contractor needs for a compliant survey

A purpose-built workspace that replaces scattered spreadsheets, word docs, and disconnected label printing software.

CRM

Client & Site Intake

Capture site addresses, primary contacts, and initial call notes. Track every opportunity on a drag-and-drop Kanban pipeline.

QST

Survey Questionnaire

Pre-survey checklist for PPE requirements, site access, and escort logistics. Draft intro emails automatically from the answers.

FLD

Panels & Equipment

Log switchboards, MCCs, and disconnects. Attach standard and thermal/infrared photos directly to the equipment record in the field.

CAL

Calculations Hub

Organize incident energy, arc flash boundary, and PPE category data. Built to seamlessly accept outputs from SKM or ETAP engineering software.

$

Mobilization Pricing

Calculate exact mobilization costs including vehicle rates, crew size, hours per trip, IRS mileage, per diem, and install minimums.

PRO

Proposal Generator

Pull field data, rate sheets, and mobilization rules into a clean PDF proposal. Includes assumptions, exclusions, and an internal executive summary.

QR

Dual-QR Labels

Print compliant labels with two QR codes: an offline data card readable with no signal, and a live link that opens the panel's full PDF via Google Drive.

LOG

Correspondence Calendar

Log every email, call, and site visit. Schedule follow-ups directly on the module's unified calendar to ensure no client slips through the cracks.

How it works

A structured workflow for hazard mitigation

1

Pipeline & Intake

Create a new project from the dashboard. Answer the site questionnaire to capture safety logistics, generating a pre-drafted field survey email for the client.

2

Field Data Collection

Walk the site with a tablet. Add panels, log voltages and bus ratings, and drop photos directly onto the equipment record. Generate a summary email for the client instantly.

3

Proposals & Pricing

Apply company-wide rate sheets to the collected data. The system calculates mobilization and installation minimums, spitting out a ready-to-send PDF proposal.

4

Engineering Review

Print the raw data for leadership review. Run calculations through your licensed engineering software (e.g., SKM, ETAP) and input the resulting incident energy and boundaries back into the platform.

5

Label Printing & Closeout

Print the final NFPA 70E labels. Check off each panel as it is physically installed on site, and generate a final completion email summarizing the project.
