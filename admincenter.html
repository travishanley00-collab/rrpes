What it does

Company-wide admin, not a scattered set of workarounds

Access control, roles, and where files live shouldn't be tribal knowledge — AdminCenter makes them a screen leadership can actually see and change.

ACC

Team Accounts

Add or deactivate people, set their role, and keep one account list instead of asking around who still needs access.

ROL

Roles

A role list that drives Team Accounts, the Estimator/Role field in Project Center, and the columns of the access matrix — one place, not three.

MTX

Screen Access by Role

Check a box to let a role see a given screen or nav group. Nothing is enforced until you explicitly turn it on — access starts fail-open, on purpose, so nobody gets locked out by accident during setup.

PIN

PIN-Gated Sensitive Centers

HR Center, Finance Center, and Admin Center itself carry their own PIN gate on top of role access — a role still needs the PIN to get past it.

ROS

Estimator / Proposal Roster

A read-only view of the sender roster from Project Center's Estimators & Sending panel — who a proposal comes from, and the role Project Center checks against the access matrix.

DIR

File Locations

Where each center reads and writes on the shared drive, under one fixed team root. Change a folder name here and every center picks it up next sync — nothing to reinstall.

LOG

Activity Log

Every admin change is logged, with a date-range view and print output across every center — not just this browser's short recent list.

SHR

Shared, Multi-User Data

One shared record everyone in the company reads from — not a settings file that only reflects whoever last opened it.

How it works

From "everyone sees everything" to "on purpose, by role"

1

Set up roles and accounts

Define the roles your operation actually uses, then add each person's account and assign their role — the same role list feeds Project Center's own Estimator/Role field.

2

Build the access matrix

Check which screens each role can see. Until you save and enforce it, the system stays fail-open — everyone sees everything — so building the matrix is a deliberate, reviewable step, not a risk of locking someone out mid-setup.

3

Turn on enforcement

Flip the enforce switch once the matrix looks right, and Project Center starts checking it — screen by screen, role by role.

4

PIN-gate the sensitive centers

HR, Finance, and Admin get an additional PIN on top of role access — a second, deliberate gate for the centers that hold the most sensitive data.

5

Point every center at the right folder

Set the shared-drive file locations once, and every center reads and writes from the same place — no separate configuration to keep in sync by hand.

Why not just manage this by hand?

Tribal knowledge isn't an access policy

Most shops manage access with a mental list of who's allowed to see what. That works until someone leaves, someone gets promoted, or someone new needs to know why they can't see a screen.
