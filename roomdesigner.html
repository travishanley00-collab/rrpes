What it does

A real layout tool, not a sketch on the back of a plan sheet

Built for cabinetry and millwork specifically — draw a room, place real catalog items, and walk away with a priced, project-ready list.

RM

Room & Wall Drawing

Draw walls freehand or drop in a rectangle room to start, then adjust wall length precisely — snapping keeps everything lined up.

CAB

Drag-and-Drop Catalog

Cabinets and appliances come from a real product catalog and library, organized by category and product line — drag one in and it snaps to the nearest wall.

BOM

Live Bill of Materials

Every cabinet placed rolls into a bill of materials with a cost sheet, kept current as the layout changes — not rebuilt by hand after the design is "done."

SND

Send to Project

Send the finished cabinet list straight into the project it belongs to — no re-typing a parts list from a drawing into the estimate.

LAY

Multiple Named Layouts

Create, duplicate, rename, and manage multiple layout options per project — compare alternatives without losing earlier versions.

UND

Undo, Zoom & Precise Placement

Full undo/redo, zoom and pan, and per-cabinet height controls while designing — mistakes are a keystroke away from fixed, not a redraw.

EXP

CSV Export & Printable Reports

Export the cabinet list and cost sheet to CSV, or print a report directly from the layout — for a client, a vendor, or the shop floor.

SHR

Shared, Multi-User Data

Layouts and the product catalog live in the same shared record as the rest of the platform — not a file saved to one designer's desktop.

How it works

From "empty room" to "priced cabinet list in the project"

1

Draw the room

Sketch the walls freehand or start from a rectangle room, then dial in exact wall lengths.

2

Drag cabinets and appliances in

Pull items from the catalog and library by category or product line — they snap to the nearest wall automatically as you place them.

3

Fine-tune the layout

Rotate, reposition, and adjust cabinet heights, with full undo/redo and zoom while you work through the details.

4

The bill of materials builds itself

Every placed item rolls into a priced BOM and cost sheet automatically — no separate tally after the fact.

5

Send it to the project

One click sends the finished cabinet list into the project it belongs to, or export it to CSV or a printed report for a client or vendor.

Why not just use a generic CAD tool?

General-purpose CAD doesn't know your catalog

A drafting program can draw a room too — but it doesn't know your product line, doesn't price anything, and doesn't talk to your estimate.
